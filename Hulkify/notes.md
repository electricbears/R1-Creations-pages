## Prompt Pattern Analysis

DomKamForever's prompts follow consistent structures:

- **Start with "Take a picture"** or **"Take a picture in the style of"** to establish the transformation
- **Specify the target style or transformation** (cyberpunk, LEGO, historical period, etc.)
- **Add descriptive detail** about the desired aesthetic (colors, materials, techniques)
- **Include specific instructions** where needed (maintain characteristics, add elements, photorealistic, 8k resolution)
- **Keep sentences concise but descriptive** enough for the LLM to understand the intent
